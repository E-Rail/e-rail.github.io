03 / NOT DOWNLOADED

External pages stay external.

User action is required

Operator pages and files open only after the rider taps a link. Just-Go does not bundle, prefetch, or redistribute them, and they are never counted as offline content.

  • The directory’s 770 records comprise 275 Maps, 447 Travel, 6 Accessibility, and 42 Help links across 698 unique targets.
  • Hong Kong’s external MTR targets are 1 System Map, 98 Location Maps, 98 Station Layouts, and 14 Light Rail Street Maps—not included map files.
  • Attribution, license, and provider links behave the same way: opening them contacts the destination only after a user action.
  • If an external destination changes or disappears, Just-Go does not retain a hidden offline copy.

04 / ONLINE-ONLY BEHAVIOR

Live means a provider request.

Hong Kong has live-arrival references for all 162 matched stations. Asking for current arrivals contacts the DATA.GOV.HK government transport API. The returned current values are not part of the offline pack and should not be described as static schedules.

First and last trains for Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Hangzhou work the same way: they are read from the operator when you open a station, not shipped in the pack. A saved snapshot covers you when the operator cannot be reached.

Place search and walking approaches may also require Apple services and connectivity. Included metro network geometry does not make every part of trip planning offline.

05 / SAVED ON YOUR DEVICE

A cached snapshot is not a city pack.

Opening a station detail page for Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, or Hangzhou fetches that station’s information from the operator and keeps the last good response on your device: backup-excluded, capped at 1 MB per station, and wiped by Settings → Clear Cache.

That snapshot exists so the station page still answers when you are underground or offline, where it appears labeled as saved and dated. It is not downloaded Just-Go content, is never merged into a city pack, and is never redistributed.
