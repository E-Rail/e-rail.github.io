01 / SCOPE

Terms for using Just-Go.

By downloading, building, using, or relying on Just-Go or its hosted documentation, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the service.

The MIT License separately governs Just-Go’s original source code. Third-party data, services, names, trademarks, and media retain their own terms.

02 / THE SERVICE

Informational transit tools.

Just-Go provides map-first place discovery, metro route planning across supported networks, walking and other first/last-mile legs, route comparison, station details, accessibility context, Quick Tags, local trip records, operator information, and Hong Kong live-arrival lookups.

Just-Go is an independent open-source project. It is not a transit operator, emergency service, accessibility certification body, official navigation product, or ticketing service.

04 / SAFETY

Verify before and during travel.

Follow the source closest to the railway

Operator notices, station signage, platform displays, announcements, barriers, staff, and emergency authorities take priority over Just-Go.

  • Time-sensitive tripsDo not use Just-Go as the sole source for last-train, disruption, evacuation, medical, or other high-consequence decisions.
  • AccessibilityAn absent record is not proof that a facility is unavailable; a present record can still change or be out of service.
  • Walking, cycling, and drivingApple-provided routes and estimates can be incomplete, unavailable, or unsuitable for local conditions.
  • Indoor movementJust-Go ships no indoor-navigation runtime and provides no verified transfer-path, boarding-zone, or door guidance.
