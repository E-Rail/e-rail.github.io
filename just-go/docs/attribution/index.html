04 / MAINLAND OPERATORS

Identifiers and recipes, not copied content.

Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Hangzhou station references include reviewed operator identifiers, canonical station mappings, aliases, source URLs, and Just-Go-authored request/mapping recipes. Operator station text, schedules, facilities, exits, descriptions, images, and layouts are not republished as a Just-Go service.

05 / EXTERNAL RESOURCES

770 links remain external.

The reviewed catalog contains 770 link records resolving to 698 unique targets across 43 cities. Linked pages, PDFs, images, maps, and other resources remain with their providers. A user-initiated link does not copy the destination into Just-Go or change its license.

06 / UNIVERSAL CITY FORMAT

One document, the full source union.

Each of the 58 developer-facing universal city documents combines authored schema fields with the city’s applicable network, station, official-resource, coverage, and rights records. The files are integrity-indexed repository artifacts and are not bundled into the app.

No license flattening

Publishing one JSON shape does not place every field under one license. Each city document carries rights identifiers, and downstream consumers must preserve the terms attached to its sources.
